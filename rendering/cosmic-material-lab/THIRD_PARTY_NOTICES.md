The cosmic GLSL and ten animated grayscale cosmic sprites derive from the user-provided Re-Avaritia-forge-1.20.1 snapshot, ZIP SHA-256 5542f323b47eb6555764c30f0776ca2d0045b52dc13a7294bc99bb5db6b71ce0. Its MIT license is retained in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/Re-Avaritia-MIT.txt.

The lab preserves the direction-driven 16-layer field and changes the final composition to a single item pass using original texture alpha, optional red-channel mask, explicit palette and safe nonzero uniform defaults. Java adapters and the validation harness are new work. Minecraft base textures are referenced from the installed game; they are not bundled. The blade mask was generated from cyan-region classification of the installed diamond sword texture.
